## What are push notifications?

Push notifications land right on your customers' lock screen — just like a text message or a WhatsApp. The best part: your customers don't need an app for it. The message goes out automatically to everyone who has saved your digital stamp card to their wallet.

That gives you a direct, personal line to your regulars — no flyers, no social media, no wasted reach.

## Step by step: sending a push notification

1. Open your **dashboard** at [bonuskarte.digital](https://bonuskarte.digital)
2. Click **Mailings** in the left-hand menu
3. Click **New message**
4. Pick your **audience** (all customers, or a specific segment)
5. Write your **message** — keep it short and punchy, two or three sentences at most
6. Choose whether to **send now** or **schedule it** for later
7. Click **Send** or **Schedule**

<Callout type="tipp">Schedule your messages for the right moment — for example at 11:30, just before your guests start thinking about lunch.</Callout>

## Best practices: how to make your messages land

- **One or two messages per week, tops** — less really is more
- **Timing is everything**: send before lunch (11:30) or in the early evening (17:00)
- **Always offer something of value**: an offer, a tip, an invitation — never spam
- **Keep it personal**: "We baked fresh cake today" beats "Offer of the week" every time
- **Short and punchy**: your message should make its point in one or two sentences

<Callout type="warnung">Too many push notifications will push customers to delete your card from their wallet. Stick to one or two messages a week, and only send when you genuinely have something to say.</Callout>

## Geolocation push: reaching customers nearby

With the geolocation feature, you can automatically send a message the moment a customer comes near your business (roughly a 100-metre radius). Perfect for catching spontaneous passers-by.

Example: _"You're just around the corner — drop in and grab a coffee on the house from 5 stamps!"_

<Callout type="info">Geolocation only works if the customer has enabled location access for their wallet. Most people have this switched on by default.</Callout>
