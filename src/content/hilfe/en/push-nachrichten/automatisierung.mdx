## Why automate?

Automated push notifications run in the background — you set them up once and they work for you. That way you reach your customers at exactly the right moment, without having to think about it every day.

## Available automations

### 1. Welcome message

**Trigger:** A customer saves your stamp card to their wallet.

Greet new customers right away with a friendly message. For example: _"Welcome aboard! Great to have you. On your next visit you'll get your first stamp straight away."_

### 2. Inactivity reminder

**Trigger:** A customer hasn't collected a stamp for X days.

Win back lapsed customers before they forget about you. For example: _"We miss you! Stop by and grab your next stamp."_

<Callout type="tipp">Set the window to 14 to 30 days — that's the sweet spot for bringing customers back before they drift away for good.</Callout>

### 3. Birthday greeting

**Trigger:** It's the customer's birthday.

Surprise your customers with a personal birthday message. For example: _"Happy birthday! Your next coffee is on us."_

### 4. Almost-full card

**Trigger:** A customer has collected X of Y stamps.

Give customers who are close to the reward a nudge. For example: _"Just 2 stamps to go until your free coffee — come see us soon!"_

### 5. Geolocation push

**Trigger:** A customer is within roughly 100 metres.

Reach customers who happen to be nearby. For example: _"You're right around the corner — we've got fresh croissants today!"_

## How to set up an automation

1. Open your **dashboard** at [bonuskarte.digital](https://bonuskarte.digital)
2. Click **Mailings** in the left-hand menu
3. Select the **Automations** tab
4. Click **New automation**
5. Choose the **trigger** you want (e.g. inactivity)
6. **Define the condition** (e.g. "after 21 days without a visit")
7. Write your **message**
8. Click **Activate**

<Callout type="tipp">Start with two automations: the welcome message and the inactivity reminder. These two have the biggest impact on customer retention.</Callout>

<Callout type="info">Automations run in the background and fire only once per customer — except for inactivity, which is sent again whenever the customer becomes inactive once more.</Callout>
