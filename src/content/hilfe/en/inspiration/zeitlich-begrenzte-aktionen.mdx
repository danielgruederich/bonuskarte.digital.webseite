With **Promotions** you can run time-limited offers directly on your existing customer card — no need to build a new card from scratch. The feature works with every card type and is especially effective for seasonal moments, holidays and special events.

A little urgency goes a long way, and your audience is receptive to it: 82.1% of consumers in the DACH region want to hear about offers and news at least once a week, and 71.5% prefer to receive them through digital channels such as an app, email or website — versus just 28.5% who favour print (hello again × Handelsverband, DACH Loyalty Report 2026). A digital card with a built-in push announcement hits both marks at once.

## How to create a promotion

### 1. Set up the promotion

In your dashboard, go to **Cards → Promotions** and click **Create promotion**. Give it a name, an optional description, and adjust the custom fields.

<Callout type="tipp">
Three fields are set up by default — you can delete individual ones or add more, exactly as you need.
</Callout>

### 2. Configure push notifications

Let your customers know about the offer. You can schedule two push notifications:

- **Before the start** — for example a few days ahead as a teaser
- **Before the end** — as a reminder before the offer runs out

For each one, pick the date, write the text, and enable the notification.

### 3. Upload a card image

You can temporarily swap out your card image for the duration of the promotion — it makes the offer even more visible and effective. Upload your promotion image and click **Apply**.

<Callout type="info">
The image format for promotions differs slightly from regular card images. You'll find the exact dimensions in the knowledge base.
</Callout>

### 4. Set the time frame

Set the **start and end date** of your promotion.

### 5. Set limits (optional)

You can cap how often the promotion may be used:

- **Per customer** — e.g. a maximum of 1 redemption per person
- **Total** — e.g. a maximum of 100 redemptions overall

<Callout type="tipp">
If you don't want any limits, simply leave the fields blank — there's nothing you need to enter there.
</Callout>

### 6. Select a card and activate

Choose the card the promotion should run on and click **Activate**. If you're not sure yet, save it as a **draft** first — you can edit it any time and activate it later.

## What happens after the promotion?

As soon as the time frame ends or the set limit is reached, all cards return to their normal view **automatically** — you don't have to do a thing.

<Callout type="info">
If a customer hits their individual limit, their card is reset to the normal view straight away and the promotion can no longer be used.
</Callout>

## When is a promotion worth running?

- **Holidays** — Christmas, Easter, Valentine's Day
- **Seasonal campaigns** — summer special, winter push
- **Events** — anniversary, grand opening, town festival
- **Flash sales** — short, high-pressure offers, e.g. "This weekend only"

<Callout type="tipp">
Pair a flash sale with the "before the end" push notification. A final reminder in the last hours of an offer is often what turns an intention into an actual visit.
</Callout>
