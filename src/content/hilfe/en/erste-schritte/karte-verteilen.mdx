The more customers have your stamp card, the more often they come back. Here are the best ways to get your card out into the world.

## Put up a QR code

The QR code is the easiest way for customers to load your card onto their phone. One quick scan — and the card is saved in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

**The best spots for your QR code:**

- **Till / counter** — right where people pay (highest attention)
- **Table display** — customers can scan at their leisure while they wait
- **Menu** — as a small note on the last page or the back
- **Front door / shop window** — even passers-by get curious
- **Pickup counter** — perfect for takeaway customers

<Callout type="tipp">
A short line next to the QR code works wonders: "Load your stamp card onto your phone — get [your reward] free on your 10th visit!" That way customers immediately know what's in it for them.
</Callout>

## Use your share link

You can use your personal share link anywhere you talk to customers online:

- **WhatsApp** — send the link to regulars or share it in your WhatsApp group
- **Instagram** — put the link in your bio or share it in a Story
- **Facebook** — post the link on your page with a short note
- **Google Business Profile** — add the link as your website or under "Updates"
- **Email signature** — attach the link to every email you send

<Callout type="info">
You'll find your share link in the email you received when you started. If you can't locate it, just drop us a message and we'll send it again.
</Callout>

## Tips for more installs

### Talk to customers directly

Nothing beats a personal conversation: *"Have you seen our stamp card yet? Just scan the QR code — get a free coffee on your 10th visit."* It takes 10 seconds and works better than any poster.

### Give a welcome stamp

Give new customers a stamp right on their first visit. It lowers the barrier to getting started and gives them a reason to come back.

<Callout type="tipp">
A welcome stamp is the best icebreaker. When customers see they already have a stamp, they're not starting from zero — and they're more likely to return.
</Callout>

### Brief your team

Take a moment to explain to your team how the card works. When everyone knows the ropes, the card gets offered far more often. Five minutes is all it takes.

### Boost visibility

- Put the QR code up in **several places**, not just one
- Change its position now and then — a fresh spot catches more eyes
- Run promotions like "This week: double stamps!" to draw attention
