Great to have you on board. Your digital stamp card is fully set up and ready for your customers. On this page you'll learn what's included and how to get going in just a few minutes.

## What's included

Signing up with bonuskarte.digital gives you everything you need:

- **Your digital stamp card** — already designed with your logo, your colours and your reward
- **A QR code** — so your customers can load the card onto their phone
- **A share link** — to send via WhatsApp, Instagram or email
- **Access to the scanner app** — for handing out stamps and redeeming rewards

<Callout type="info">
Your login details for the scanner app were sent to you by email. Be sure to check your spam folder if you can't find them.
</Callout>

## Ready in 3 steps

### 1. Open the scanner app

Open the **scanner app** on your phone and sign in with your login details. That's it — the app is ready to use straight away.

<Callout type="tipp">
Install the app on your team members' phones too. That way everyone on your team can hand out stamps.
</Callout>

### 2. Print and place your QR code

Print your QR code and put it somewhere clearly visible — ideally at the till, on the counter or as a table display. The more visible it is, the more customers will load the card.

### 3. Hand out your first stamp

Open the scanner app, scan the QR code on your customer's phone and hand out the first stamp. Done!

<Callout type="tipp">
Try the whole flow yourself once: scan your own QR code with your personal phone, load the card into your Wallet and give yourself a test stamp. That way you'll know exactly how it looks for your customers.
</Callout>
