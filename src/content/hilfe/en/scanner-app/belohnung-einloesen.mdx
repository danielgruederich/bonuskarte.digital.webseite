# Redeeming a reward

When a customer has collected all their stamps, it's time for the reward. Here's how to redeem it and what happens next.

## How it works

1. **The customer shows you their full card** — on their phone you can see that every stamp field is filled
2. **Open the scanner app** and tap "Scan"
3. **Scan the QR code** on your customer's phone
4. **Tap "Redeem"** — the reward is marked as redeemed
5. **Give the customer their reward** — for example the free coffee, the dessert or the discount

<Callout type="info">
The customer doesn't need to do anything else. After redeeming, their card is reset automatically and they can start collecting stamps again from scratch.
</Callout>

## Automatic vs. manual redemption

### Automatic redemption (default)

In the default mode, the reward is **activated automatically** as soon as the last stamp is added. The customer sees straight away on their card that the reward is ready. On their next visit, you scan the card and redeem the reward.

### Manual redemption

With manual redemption, you actively confirm the reward in the app. This gives you more control — for example when you want to make sure the reward is only redeemed on site.

<Callout type="tipp">
For most cafés and restaurants, automatic redemption is the simplest option. The customer arrives with a full card, you scan it and hand over the reward — done.
</Callout>

## What happens after redemption?

After the reward is redeemed, your customer's stamp card is **reset automatically**. All stamps are cleared and the customer starts fresh with an empty card. That keeps them motivated to carry on collecting right away.

<Callout type="warnung">
The card is reset automatically after redemption. Make sure you give the customer their reward **before** you tap "Redeem" in the app — after that, the stamps are gone.
</Callout>

## Tips for everyday use

- **Make redemption an experience** — a quick "Congratulations, you made it!" creates a good mood
- **Remind the customer to keep collecting** — "Your card is fresh again, and your next visit earns the first stamp!"
- **Brief your team** — every team member should know how redemption works so customers don't have to wait
