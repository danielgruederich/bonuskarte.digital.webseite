# Scanner in kiosk mode

With the scanner app's kiosk mode you can simplify your workflow, avoid duplicate scans and hand out loyalty points automatically — straight from your computer, tablet or smartphone.

## Where to find kiosk mode

You'll find kiosk mode in the top right, in the hamburger menu under your manager profile.

<Callout type="info">
The scanner app is web-based and runs directly in your browser — no installation needed. You can still save it to your device's home screen for faster access.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tipp">
Every manager should sign in with their own login details.
</Callout>

## Setting up kiosk mode

In kiosk mode, you define the rules for handing out stamps or points automatically. You also set how much time should pass between two scans — for example 10 or 20 seconds.

This prevents duplicate transactions when the same card is scanned again shortly after.

The list shows every card in your account. You can enable kiosk mode for a single card or for all cards at once.

<Callout type="info">
If a card can't be enabled, that card type isn't supported in kiosk mode.
</Callout>

## How to start kiosk mode

1. Click **Start** — the camera is activated
2. Scan your customer's card
3. The transaction runs automatically

In the recent transactions, you'll see that the visit was recorded correctly.

## Important notes

<Callout type="warnung">
Setting this up requires the main account's login details.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
In kiosk mode, no notes can be added to a transaction.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warnung">
If the device is switched off or logged out, kiosk mode is disabled and has to be turned back on manually.
</Callout>

With kiosk mode, your loyalty process becomes faster, simpler and more reliable.
