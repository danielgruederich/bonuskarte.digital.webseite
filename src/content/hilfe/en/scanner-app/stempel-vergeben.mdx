With the scanner app you can hand out stamps in just a few seconds. Here's a step-by-step walkthrough of how it works.

## Before you start

Make sure that:

- The **scanner app** is installed on your phone
- You're **signed in** to the app (using the login details from your welcome email)

## How to hand out a stamp

1. **Open the scanner app** on your phone
2. **Tap "Scan"** — the camera opens
3. **Scan the QR code** on your customer's phone (the customer opens their stamp card in the Wallet for this)
4. **The stamp is added** — you'll see a confirmation on your screen

That's it! The customer sees the new stamp on their card right away.

<Callout type="tipp">
The whole process takes under 5 seconds. It's worth practising once so it runs smoothly in day-to-day service.
</Callout>

## Handing out several stamps at once

Sometimes you'll want to give a customer more than one stamp — for example when they order two meals, or as a welcome bonus.

Here's how: after scanning, you can **adjust the number of stamps** in the app before confirming. Just enter the number you want and confirm.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the app work offline?

The scanner app needs an internet connection to hand out stamps. Make sure your phone is connected to Wi-Fi or has mobile data switched on.

<Callout type="warnung">
Without an internet connection, no stamps can be handed out. Make sure your Wi-Fi at the till is stable.
</Callout>

### Can I undo a stamp?

A stamp that has already been given can't be removed directly through the app. If a stamp was added by mistake, get in touch — we'll help you sort it out quickly.

### Who can hand out stamps?

Anyone with access to the scanner app. You can share the login details with your team members so your whole team can hand out stamps.

<Callout type="tipp">
Give your team members the login details and install the app on their phones. That way anyone on your team can hand out stamps — even when you're not around.
</Callout>
