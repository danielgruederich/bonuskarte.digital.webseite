# Gift Card

The digital gift card works just like a voucher with a stored balance. Your customers can gift it to someone else or keep it for themselves, and redeem the credit flexibly at your business.

## How it works

1. **Create the gift card** — Decide which amounts are available (e.g. €25, €50 or €100)
2. **Send the card** — Your customer buys the card and sends it to the recipient by link, QR code or email
3. **Save it to Wallet** — The recipient stores the gift card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet
4. **Redeem the balance** — At your business, the amount is deducted in full or in part

## Features

- **Single or multiple use** — Choose whether the balance is redeemed all at once or across several visits
- **Stripe integration** — Gift cards can be paid for online by credit card or Apple Pay
- **Gifting function** — Customers can send the card along with a personal message
- **Expiry date** — Set how long the gift card stays valid

<Callout type="tipp">
Actively promote gift cards around holidays such as Christmas, Mother's Day and other occasions. A simple sign at the register with a QR code is often enough to boost sales.
</Callout>

## Who is the gift card for?

- **Restaurants** — Dinner vouchers to give as a gift
- **Beauty and wellness salons** — Treatments as a present
- **Retail** — Flexible credit across the whole range
- **Experience providers** — Gift a class, workshop or event

<Callout type="info">
Gift cards bring you value twice over: you collect the revenue right at the moment of purchase, and the recipient discovers your business as a brand-new customer.
</Callout>
