The digital membership card replaces the traditional club pass. Your members carry their status right on their phone — with automatic renewal, different tiers and personal details.

## How it works

1. **Define the tiers** — Set up your membership levels (e.g. Basic, Premium, VIP)
2. **Issue the card** — Your customer receives their digital membership card in Wallet
3. **Show the status** — The card always displays the current tier, the member's name and, optionally, a photo
4. **Renew automatically** — The membership renews on its own, with payments handled through Stripe

## Features

- **Multiple tiers** — Define different membership levels, each with its own benefits
- **Stripe subscriptions** — Automatic monthly or yearly billing directly through the card
- **Trial period** — Offer new members a free trial to get started
- **Visit counter** — Track how often a member comes in
- **Photo and name** — The card shows the member's name and, optionally, a photo

<Callout type="warnung">
Some membership card settings (such as tiers and billing intervals) cannot be changed once the card is active. Plan your membership structure carefully before you go live.
</Callout>

## Who is the membership card for?

- **Gyms** — A digital member pass with automatic renewal
- **Coworking spaces** — Different rate plans as membership tiers
- **Clubs and associations** — A member pass in Wallet, complete with a membership number
- **Premium customer programs** — VIP cards for regulars with special perks

<Callout type="tipp">
Use the trial period to make it easy for new members to get started. A free trial week noticeably lowers the barrier to signing up.
</Callout>
