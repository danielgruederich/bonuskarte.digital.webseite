The stamp card is the classic loyalty card everyone knows — just digital. Your customers collect stamps and, once they reach a set number, they earn a reward. No paper to print, nothing to lose, everything lives right on their phone.

## How it works

1. **Install the card** — Your customer scans the QR code and saves the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet
2. **Collect stamps** — On each visit, you scan the card with the scanner app and add a stamp
3. **Earn the reward** — Once every stamp is collected, your customer redeems the reward
4. **Start over** — After redemption, the card resets automatically and your customer begins collecting again

## Features

- **Choose your own stamp count** — You decide how many stamps are needed to unlock the reward (e.g. 8, 10 or 12)
- **Welcome stamp** — Automatically award a stamp on first install to get customers off to a strong start
- **Birthday bonus** — Surprise your customers with an extra stamp or a special message on their birthday
- **Daily limit** — Cap how many stamps can be earned per day to prevent misuse
- **Multiple rewards** — Set up different rewards for different stamp milestones
- **Referral program** — Customers earn a bonus when they invite their friends

<Callout type="warnung">
The number of stamps cannot be changed once the card has been activated. Think carefully about how many stamps you want to set before you go live.
</Callout>

## Who is the stamp card for?

- **Cafés** — Buy 10 coffees, get 1 free
- **Restaurants** — 8 visits, a dessert on the house
- **Hair salons** — 6 haircuts, the next one at half price
- **Nail studios** — 5 treatments, 1 free

<Callout type="tipp">
Start with a manageable stamp count (for example, 8). Your customers see progress faster and stay motivated. You can always create a new card with more stamps later on.
</Callout>
