# Promotions

Promotions let you push time-limited offers and campaigns directly onto your customers' cards. They see the offer instantly on their card — complete with a push notification.

## How it works

1. **Create the promotion** — Define your offer, the time frame and the design
2. **Show it automatically** — The promotion appears on every active customer card on its own
3. **Send a push notification** — Your customers get a notification right on their lock screen
4. **Redeem** — Your customer shows the promotion in store and you scan it with the scanner app

## Features

- **The card design changes** — During the promotion, the card's look updates to draw attention to the offer
- **Push notifications** — Every cardholder automatically gets a message on their phone
- **Custom background** — Upload your own image for the promotion
- **Usage limit** — Cap how often the promotion can be redeemed per customer
- **Activate or deactivate anytime** — Start or stop the promotion with a single click

<Callout type="warnung">
Only one promotion can be active per card template at a time. End the current promotion before you start a new one.
</Callout>

## Who are promotions ideal for?

- **Seasonal offers** — Summer specials, holiday menus or spring promotions
- **Event announcements** — Live music, tastings or special evenings
- **Flash sales** — Short-notice deals like "Today only: 2-for-1 on all cocktails"
- **Anniversaries and birthdays** — Celebrate your business anniversary with a special offer for everyone

<Callout type="tipp">
Don't run promotions too often, or they lose their impact. One or two per month is a good rule of thumb to keep your customers engaged without wearing out their patience.
</Callout>
