The discount card rewards your customers with a discount that grows over time. The more they spend with you, the higher their personal discount becomes. It encourages repeat purchases and strengthens loyalty.

## How it works

1. **Make a purchase** — Your customer shops with you as usual
2. **Track the spend** — Their total spend is recorded automatically on the card
3. **Unlock a higher discount** — Once a spending threshold is reached, the discount moves up to the next tier automatically
4. **Use the discount** — Your customer shows the card and receives their current discount on the purchase

## Features

- **1 to 6 tiers** — Define progressive discount levels (e.g. 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%)
- **Automatic upgrades** — Customers move up to the next tier as soon as they reach the threshold
- **Happy hours** — Award double spending points at set times for faster tier progression

<Callout type="tipp">
Communicate the discount tiers clearly and visibly — for example, on a small sign at the register. When customers see how close they are to the next tier, they come back more often.
</Callout>

## Who is the discount card for?

- **Retail** — Reward regulars with a growing discount
- **Fashion stores** — Give loyal customers bigger discounts on new collections
- **Bookshops** — Keep avid readers with rising discounts
- **Wine shops** — Reward regulars with progressive discounts on wine and spirits

<Callout type="info">
How this differs from the cashback card: with the discount card, your customer gets an immediate discount on their next purchase. With the cashback card, they collect points to redeem later. Choose the model that fits your business best.
</Callout>
