# Coupon

The digital coupon is your tool for targeted campaigns and winning new customers. Your customer redeems the coupon once — and afterward you can move them into your loyalty program.

## How it works

1. **Create the offer** — Define your coupon (e.g. "20% off your first visit" or "one drink on the house")
2. **Distribute it** — Share the coupon by QR code, social media, WhatsApp or on your website
3. **Redeem once** — Your customer shows the coupon in store and you scan it with the scanner app
4. **Convert to a loyalty card** — After redemption, you can automatically offer the customer your loyalty card

## Features

- **Single use** — Each coupon can be redeemed only once per customer
- **Loyalty card link** — After redemption, the customer is automatically offered your stamp card or rewards card
- **Expiry date** — Set a deadline for the coupon to create a sense of urgency
- **Issue limit** — Cap the total number of coupons available (e.g. "only for the first 100 customers")

<Callout type="tipp">
Pair the coupon with your stamp card: the coupon gets the new customer through the door, and the stamp card makes sure they come back.
</Callout>

## Who is the coupon for?

- **Grand openings** — Create buzz and win your first customers
- **Seasonal campaigns** — Summer specials, holiday offers or spring promotions
- **Events and trade fairs** — Hand coupons to visitors and draw them into your business
- **Social media campaigns** — Exclusive offers for your followers on Instagram or Facebook

<Callout type="info">
Unlike the stamp card, the coupon is designed for a single use. It is the perfect door-opener for new customers, whom you can then keep for the long term with a loyalty program.
</Callout>
