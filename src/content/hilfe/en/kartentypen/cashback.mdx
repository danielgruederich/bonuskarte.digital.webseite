# Cashback

With the cashback card, your customers automatically earn points on every purchase, based on the amount they spend. The more they buy, the higher their cashback rate can climb.

## How it works

1. **Make a purchase** — Your customer pays as usual at your business
2. **Earn points** — A percentage of the purchase is credited automatically as points (e.g. 5% of €20 = 1 point)
3. **Climb the tiers** — The more your customer spends in total, the higher their cashback rate becomes
4. **Redeem points** — Collected points can be applied as credit toward future purchases

## Features

- **Up to 6 tiers** — Define progressive cashback rates (e.g. 3%, 5%, 8%, 10%, 12%, 15%)
- **Automatic upgrades** — Customers move up to the next tier the moment they cross the threshold
- **Point expiry** — Set how many days unused points remain valid before they expire
- **Welcome points** — Grant starter points at sign-up as an added incentive

<Callout type="tipp">
Set the first tier deliberately low so customers reach it quickly. An early win keeps them motivated and shows the value of your loyalty program right away.
</Callout>

## Who is the cashback card for?

- **Retail** — Reward regulars for their everyday purchases
- **Gas stations** — Cashback on every fill-up
- **Drugstores and cosmetics shops** — Points across the entire range
- **Delis and wine shops** — Keep loyal customers with a rising cashback rate

<Callout type="info">
The cashback card is a great fit for businesses with varying purchase values. Unlike the stamp card, it rewards spending rather than the number of visits — so customers who spend more get more back.
</Callout>
