# Multi-Pass

The multi-pass is the digital take on the classic 10-visit punch card. Your customer buys a bundle of visits up front and uses them one at a time. It is a perfect fit for services sold in fixed units.

## How it works

1. **Buy the bundle** — Your customer purchases, for example, a 10-visit pass (online or in store)
2. **Redeem visits** — On each visit you scan the card and one visit is deducted
3. **See the balance** — Your customer can check at any time how many visits are left on the card
4. **Buy again** — Once all visits are used up, your customer can purchase a new bundle

## Features

- **Flexible count** — You decide how many visits a bundle includes (5, 10, 20 or any number you like)
- **Expiry date** — Set a deadline by which the visits must be redeemed
- **Points per use** — Award bonus points on top with every redemption
- **Birthday bonus** — Treat your customers to an extra visit on their birthday

<Callout type="tipp">
Offer the bundle at an attractive price — for example, 10 visits for the price of 8. That encourages customers to commit to you for the longer term right away.
</Callout>

## Who is the multi-pass for?

- **Yoga and Pilates studios** — A 10-class pass for group sessions
- **Gyms** — Flexible bundle bookings without a subscription
- **Beauty and cosmetics salons** — Multi-treatment packages
- **Car washes** — A 5- or 10-wash pass

<Callout type="info">
The multi-pass works especially well for services with a fixed single price. That keeps the bundle easy for your customers to understand and makes the discount clear at a glance.
</Callout>
