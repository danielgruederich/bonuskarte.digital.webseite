The rewards card is the most versatile loyalty program. Your customers collect points and redeem them for a range of rewards — from small extras all the way to bigger prizes.

## How it works

1. **Collect points** — Your customers earn points on every visit or purchase
2. **Choose a reward** — Once they have enough points, they pick a reward from the catalog
3. **Redeem** — You scan the card, the points are deducted and the reward is granted
4. **Keep collecting** — Your customer immediately starts collecting again toward the next reward

## Rewards card vs. stamp card

| | Stamp card | Rewards card |
|---|---|---|
| **Collecting** | 1 stamp per visit | Flexible point allocation |
| **Rewards** | 1 fixed reward | Multiple reward tiers |
| **Redeeming** | All or nothing | Partial redemption possible |
| **Flexibility** | Simple and clear | Versatile and customizable |

## Features

- **3 ways to collect** — Points per visit, per euro spent, or awarded manually
- **Unlimited reward tiers** — Create as many rewards as you like (e.g. 50 points = coffee, 100 points = cake, 200 points = a main course)
- **3 reward types** — A free product, a percentage discount, or a fixed-amount credit
- **Point expiry** — Set how many days unused points remain valid before they expire

<Callout type="tipp">
Set the first reward tier deliberately low (reachable after just 3 to 5 visits). That early sense of achievement motivates your customers to stick with it and work toward the bigger rewards.
</Callout>

## Who is the rewards card for?

- **Restaurants with tiered rewards** — A small extra after 5 visits, a bigger gift after 20
- **Shops with several product categories** — Customers choose for themselves what to spend their points on

<Callout type="info">
Choose the rewards card when you want to offer your customers more choice than a simple stamp card allows. For a straightforward "10 stamps = 1 free" model, the stamp card is all you need.
</Callout>
