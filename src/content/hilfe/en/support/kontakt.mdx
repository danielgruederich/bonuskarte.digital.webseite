## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change my card's design?

Yes, any time. Just message us on WhatsApp or email us with the changes you'd like — and we'll take care of them for you.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Just drop us a quick message on WhatsApp, send an email or give us a call. We'll handle it.

### I forgot my password

Go to [bonuskarte.digital](https://bonuskarte.digital) and click **Forgot password**. You'll then receive an email with a link to reset it.

### My QR code isn't working

Make sure you're using the QR code from your **dashboard** — not one you created yourself or photographed. If it still doesn't work, send us a screenshot on WhatsApp and we'll help you right away.

<Callout type="info">Your current QR code is always available in your dashboard, under the menu item for your stamp card. From there you can download it or print it directly.</Callout>
